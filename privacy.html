Privacy Policy

Transparency matters. Here's how our website works.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Worth the Click ("we," "our," or "us").

Our website, https://worththeclickofficial.github.io/ , helps visitors discover useful home products, clever gadgets, and gift ideas.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit our website or interact with external links.

2. Information We Collect

Our current website does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to browse its content.

If you contact us, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and message.

Our hosting provider and other services involved in delivering the website may automatically process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs, for security and service operation.

3. How Information Is Used

Information received through visitor inquiries may be used to respond to messages and provide support.

Technical information may be processed by service providers to operate, secure, and maintain the website.

4. Affiliate Links and Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Worth the Click may include affiliate links to products offered by Amazon and other retailers.

When you click an affiliate link, you may be redirected to a third-party website. The retailer or its service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to measure referrals and purchases, subject to their own privacy policies.

We may earn a commission from qualifying purchases, at no additional cost to you.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The current version of our website does not intentionally install its own advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

Third-party websites and services that you access through our links may use cookies or other tracking technologies according to their own policies.

If we introduce additional analytics, advertising, or tracking services in the future, we will update this policy and implement any required consent controls.

6. Pinterest and Social Media

Worth the Click may share product discoveries, images, and links through Pinterest and other social media platforms.

If you interact with our content on those platforms, the platform may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy.

We may use authorized tools and integrations to manage our own published content. We do not currently offer visitors the ability to connect their Pinterest accounts through this website.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not operate a business that sells visitor personal information.

Information may be processed by hosting providers, communication services, and other service providers as necessary to operate the website.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention and Your Choices

If you contact us, we may retain your correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, or meet applicable obligations.

You may contact us to ask questions about information you have provided or request its deletion, subject to applicable requirements and exceptions.

9. External Websites

Our website may contain links to Amazon, Pinterest, and other external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites. Please review their policies before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Worth the Click is a general-interest product discovery website and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through this website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please visit our Contact page.
